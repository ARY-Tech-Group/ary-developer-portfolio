# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in ARY-Tech-Group projects to be a welcoming, respectful experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain feedback clearly and respectfully.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, intimidation, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Contact the project maintainers through the contact details listed in the repository or organization profile. Maintainers should handle reports respectfully and confidentially.

This policy may be updated as the community grows.
